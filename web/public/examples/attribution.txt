Downloaded Wikimedia's 1280px thumbnail, preserving the full composition and
existing image content. No watermark was removed. lexus-lx570.jpg is the source;
lexus-detections.png adds actual model annotations. No synthetic boxes or scores.
The German vehicle has no Moroccan plate; the false plate detection on the side
step and SUV classification as truck are deliberately retained, not ground truth.

One anonymous production API job on 15 November 2026, all three object types,
40% confidence, returned four detections at 1280 x 915 pixels. PNG, JSON and CSV
are the byte-for-byte downloaded results of that same job. The job was deleted
after retrieval. These deliberately published static samples do not expire;
JSON's original expires_at records the historical temporary job, not this copy.
Live visitor results remain private and expire after thirty minutes.

JSON retains the actual model digests, runtime, settings and raw identities.
CSV retains the service's stable twelve-column image-export schema.
This is a demonstration of model output, not an accuracy or performance benchmark.
